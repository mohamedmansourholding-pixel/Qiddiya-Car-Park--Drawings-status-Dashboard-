Every KPI, chart, and table row here is read directly from the "Submission" sheet (376 actual submission records) — Discipline, Type, Review Status, Revision, Revision Date and Date Modified all come from that sheet, not from LIST-Drawings. Two charts compare both sheets on purpose: "Status by Discipline" shows, per discipline, a bar stacked by true Review Status (Approved / Approved w/ Comments / Pending, uncapped) next to a Total Expected reference bar from the "LIST-Drawings" sheet (1,848 planned drawings, manually corrected for Surveying/Mapping — see note below). "Overdue" flags a drawing still in Pending review more than 14 days after its revision (submission) date — a fallback definition, since the Submission sheet doesn't carry a planned/due date of its own. The top banner ("Submitted so far") compares the full Submission sheet (376) against the full LIST-Drawings scope (1,848) project-wide, independent of the filters below. "Monthly Submission Trend" is plotted by Date Modified (last workflow update), not Revision Date — Revision Date tops out in June 2026 in the source file, while Date Modified extends into July 2026 and better reflects actual submission activity.
